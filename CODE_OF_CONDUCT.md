# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the MADVID community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- focusing on the product rather than personal differences
- helping others improve the project

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. Unacceptable behavior may result in removal from the project.
